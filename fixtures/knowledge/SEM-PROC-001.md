# Draft review workflow — sample

**DEMO ONLY — describes the proposed app workflow, not a current banking process.**

1. An engineer prepares a Markdown change proposal.
2. The app validates metadata and shows a diff.
3. A reviewer marks the proposal ready; later, it is sent to a GitLab merge request.
4. Authorized team members decide whether to merge.
5. Only approved merged documents are eligible for optional publication to Confluence.

An AI suggestion never automatically becomes an approved specification.
